## 🎮 About the App

**JUEGALO - Gana Dinero Real** is a mobile rewards app where users in Latin America earn real money by completing surveys, watching rewarded videos, and engaging with offers. Users accumulate coins and cash out via PayPal once they reach the minimum threshold.

| Argentina | Secondary |

**Why LatAm?**
Latin America is one of the fastest-growing mobile markets globally, with high engagement in reward apps and strong demand for accessible income opportunities. JUEGALO is built specifically for this audience — Spanish-first, PayPal cashout, and mobile-optimized for mid-range devices.

## 🏆 Why Adjoe?

JUEGALO has a dedicated **"Games & Offers"** tab currently showing "Coming Soon." We are building toward integrating an offerwall that allows users to:

- Install and play sponsored games to earn coins
- Complete in-app milestones for larger rewards
- Discover new apps relevant to their interests

Adjoe's **Playtime** model aligns perfectly with our user base — users who are motivated, engaged, and accustomed to completing tasks for rewards.
